        How TrackLine AI Works (30-Second Guide)

        1. What is a "Guardrail"?

        A Guardrail is a strict rule that keeps your script (and any AI assistant) from drifting off-brand. When you export your System Prompt, these rules tell AI models exactly what talking points to include and what vocabulary to ban.

        2. Why write in "Beats" instead of one long page?

        Breaking your video into 3–5 time-mapped beats (*Hook, Core Content, CTA*) prevents rambling intros. Our WPM slider calculates estimated speaking time for each beat automatically.

        3. What is Hook A / Hook B variation testing?

        Top creators never film just one opening hook. On Segment 1, click **Hook B** to write and test an alternative opening angle without losing your main script.
